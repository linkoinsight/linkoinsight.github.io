Help

Licences for the readers

Every reader needs Power BI Pro, as long as the capacity is smaller than F64, and a paid Business Central licence. Team Member is enough. We don't charge per reader, but Microsoft does.

Fabricado's app isn't online yet, so there is nothing to install from us today. This page is about Microsoft's products.

Updated 6 November 2026.

Which licences are needed

Licences for report readers, with Microsoft's list prices on 1 November 2026
LicenceWho needs itPrice a month
Power BI Pro Everyone who reads reports, as long as the capacity is smaller than F64. SEK 133.80 per reader, billed yearly.
Business Central Team Member Anyone who reads Business Central data in Power BI and doesn't already hold a paid Business Central licence. SEK 76.50 per reader, billed yearly.
The Fabric licence (Free) Assigned automatically the first time you sign in to Fabric. No cost.

Ten readers with Pro cost SEK 1,338 a month. If they have no Business Central licence, add SEK 765. Microsoft's list prices on 1 November 2026, excluding VAT.

From F64 on, readers can do without Pro, but F64 costs about SEK 52,600 a month with a one-year reservation. Who needs Power BI Pro? goes through the exceptions.

Sources

Microsoft on Fabric licences, Microsoft's prices for Power BI and Microsoft's prices for Business Central.
