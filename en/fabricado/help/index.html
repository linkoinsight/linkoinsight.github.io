Help and setup guide

The steps at Microsoft can be done today. Fabricado's app isn't online yet, so the guide is for those who want to understand what it takes or get ready.

The setup guide

One page for each thing you need from Microsoft. Each page says who does the step and how to tell it's done.

In the app, the guide will become a checklist that shows what is done and what is waiting for someone.

  • Getting a Fabric capacity

    The trial or a paid F2 in Azure, who does what, and what can stop you.

  • Approving Fabricado's app

    What the administrator sees, which permissions the app asks for, and how to remove it again.

  • The fetch from Business Central

    What the pipeline fetches, what must be in place in Business Central, and how to tell it worked.

  • Licences for the readers

    Power BI Pro and a Business Central licence, and when readers can do without Pro.

  • Known errors

    Errors we have run into ourselves or read about at Microsoft, with what they mean and what to do.

How Fabricado will work shows the whole chain, and Answers covers the most common questions about cost, licences and Azure.

Who does what

In a small company, one person may hold all of these roles.

Global administrator

Approves Fabricado's app, once for the organisation and once for Business Central.

Fabric administrator

Looks after the settings in Fabric and gives access to the capacity.

The owner of the Azure subscription

Only needed when you buy a paid capacity.

Your Business Central partner

Often holds the admin roles already, and can help with the fetch from Business Central.

Support

Planned

Once Fabricado can be bought, this will apply:

  • We answer email within one working day, weekdays except Swedish public holidays.
  • An error we haven't seen before is fixed or explained within 48 hours.
  • Answers written with the help of AI say so.

Until then: write to linkoinsight@gmail.com.

Accessibility

The site is built towards WCAG 2.2 AA. Every page is tested automatically with axe, from 1440 pixels wide down to a 320-pixel phone. The site works without JavaScript, and colour never stands alone: it always has a word and a shape beside it. Automated tests don't find everything, so tell us if something doesn't work for you.
