Approving Fabricado's app

A Global administrator approves Fabricado's app once for the organisation, and once more for Business Central. The cost page asks for access to Azure separately. The dialog shows "Unverified", because Fabricado isn't a verified publisher with Microsoft. The app isn't online yet, so this page describes how it will work.

Planned

Updated 6 November 2026.

Two approvals, and one more question on the cost page

For the organisation

A dialog from Microsoft lists the permissions for Microsoft Graph, Fabric and storage. It says "Unverified" and "Accept for your organization". We have tried this step in our own trial.

For Business Central

A second approval, only needed in an organisation with Business Central.

On the cost page

A question of its own about Azure, only when someone opens the cost page. The dialog says "Access Azure Service Management as you".

The permissions

All are delegated: the app reaches only what the signed-in person reaches. This is the list we plan, and it may change before the app is released.

Permissions Fabricado's app will ask for, and why
PermissionWhy
Microsoft Graph: User.Read Knowing who is signed in.
Fabric: Workspace.ReadWrite.All, Item.ReadWrite.All, Item.Execute.All, Connection.ReadWrite.All Creating the workspace and its items, running the jobs and connecting them to Business Central.
Fabric: Capacity.Read.All Seeing which capacities exist.
Fabric: Tenant.Read.All Reading the organisation's settings in Fabric. For Fabric administrators only.
Azure Storage: user_impersonation Reading receipts and results in OneLake.
Business Central: Financials.ReadWrite.All Reading Business Central from the browser. It is the only delegated permission Microsoft documents for Business Central, so there is no read-only version.
Azure Service Management: user_impersonation Reading the cost, setting a budget and pausing the capacity on the cost page. It is only asked for there.

Removing the app again

An administrator removes Fabricado under Enterprise applications in Microsoft Entra, and Fabricado's jobs in your workspace. Then Fabricado can't do anything more in your environment. What you already have stays. Security and data says more.

If it stops

AADSTS650052

A service the app needs doesn't exist in the organisation yet. A Global administrator signs in once at app.fabric.microsoft.com and then approves the app again.

Conditional Access stops the sign-in

A Global administrator exempts Fabricado from the Conditional Access rule.

The app is turned off

A Global administrator turns Fabricado on under Enterprise applications in Entra.
