The fetch from Business Central

A pipeline in your own Fabric fetches the general ledger and the trial balance from Business Central's analytics API into a lakehouse in your environment. It is built and has run on Microsoft's demo company, not on any customer's data. Sales, purchases and inventory come with their packages.

Planned

Updated 6 November 2026.

What must be in place

  • Business Central online. Fabricado isn't built for your own servers.
  • Microsoft's extension for the Power BI reports in the environment. That is where the analytics API lives.
  • Microsoft's Power BI apps set up once. Otherwise the hourly job "Update Power BI Dimension Set Entries" doesn't exist, and the dimensions arrive empty.
  • The fields "Finance Start Date" and "Finance End Date" in Power BI Reports Setup blank. They cut rows away without showing it, in the balance sheet too, and the opening balances then come out wrong.
  • An app registration in your own Microsoft Entra, entered on the Microsoft Entra applications page in Business Central with the permission set D365 READ. That is what keeps the fetch to reading.
  • A secret for the app registration, which stays with you. It lasts 12 months at most, and when it expires the fetch stops.

We are looking for a route without a secret. It isn't tested yet, and Microsoft's mirroring may become such a route.

What is fetched

  • General ledger entries for the balance sheet and the income statement.
  • Business Central's own trial balance, which becomes the answer key.
  • The chart of accounts and the accounting periods.
  • The dimensions.

The pipeline runs in your workspace, with your permissions. None of it passes through Fabricado's own service. Mirroring or your own data path explains why we aren't waiting for Microsoft's mirroring.

How to tell it worked

After the run, the check, Bryggan, writes a receipt in the lakehouse. It says how many months match, and explains every difference with proof. On the demo company that was 24 months and 4,361 ledger lines, with SEK 0.00 unexplained.

The fetch would rather stop than guess. If an entry's Source Code field is empty, if the dimension table is empty though entries use dimensions, or if an account's class and category disagree, the run stops and says why.

Sources

Microsoft on Business Central and Power BI and Microsoft's connector for Business Central. We read the fields and the job in the code of Microsoft's Power BI extension (BCApps).
