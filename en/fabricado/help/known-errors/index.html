Known errors in Fabric and Business Central

These are the errors we have run into ourselves or read about at Microsoft, with what they mean and what to do. Each entry says how we know. The list grows as we find more.

Fabricado's app isn't online yet, so there is nothing to install from us today. This page is about Microsoft's products.

Updated 6 November 2026.

The errors

"A Fabric trial isn't available for your account"

Microsoft refuses the trial. The likely cause is that the organisation is too new: support answers on Microsoft Q&A say about 90 days, which Microsoft doesn't document.

What to do: Wait, or buy a paid F2 in Azure.

We ran into this ourselves on 1 November 2026, in a newly created organisation.

The "Start trial" button isn't there

The setting that allows the trial is turned off.

What to do: A Fabric administrator turns on "Users can try Microsoft Fabric paid features" under Tenant settings.

Microsoft's documentation.

The dialog says "Unverified"

That isn't an error. Fabricado isn't a verified publisher with Microsoft, so the dialog says so.

What to do: You decide for yourselves whether to approve. The security page says what the app may do.

We have seen the dialog in our own trial.

AADSTS650052

A service the app needs doesn't exist in the organisation yet.

What to do: A Global administrator signs in once at app.fabric.microsoft.com and then approves the app again.

We ran into the version where the app's own service was missing on 2 November 2026.

A guest account can't administer the capacity

A partner who signs in as a guest can't be a capacity administrator.

What to do: Sign in with an account that belongs to the organisation itself.

Microsoft's documentation.

Azure refuses a company with its own domain but no Microsoft environment

The company has ended up in an "unmanaged" Microsoft environment. It is rare among Business Central customers.

What to do: Ask your partner for help before you create the Azure subscription. It can't be solved from Fabricado.

Read at Microsoft. We haven't run into it ourselves.

A schedule stops running

A schedule belongs to whoever created or last changed it. It expires after 90 days without the owner signing in to Fabric, and about ten failed runs in a row switch it off.

What to do: Let a service account own the schedule, with the Contributor role on the workspace.

Microsoft's documentation.

LSROBOTokenFailure at night

Night runs can fail with this error when the person who created the pipeline changes their password.

What to do: Let a service account make the last change to the pipeline.

Reported by others. We haven't tested it yet.

The dimensions arrive empty

Business Central fills the dimension table with an hourly job, and the job only exists once the Power BI apps are set up.

What to do: Set up Microsoft's Power BI apps once and wait for the job. Fabricado's fetch stops and says so.

We saw this on the demo company on 1 November 2026.

Opening balances come out wrong

The fields "Finance Start Date" and "Finance End Date" in Power BI Reports Setup cut rows away, in the balance sheet too. No API shows that the filter exists.

What to do: Clear both fields.

Read in the code of Microsoft's Power BI extension.

December differs from the trial balance

That is normal after the year-end close: the trial balance leaves the closing entries out of the month. The check, Bryggan, recognises them by their Source Code field and marks them as explained.

What to do: Nothing, as long as every difference is a proven closing entry.

We saw this on the demo company on 1 November 2026.

An import says it succeeded, but something is missing

Microsoft's bulk import can report "succeeded despite failures".

What to do: Don't trust the answer: check that every item is there.

Microsoft's documentation.

Have you run into something else? Get in touch, and we will add it.

Sources

Microsoft on the trial, Microsoft on admin consent and Microsoft on workspace identity.
