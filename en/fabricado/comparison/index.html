Fabricado, Microsoft's own apps or a consultant

Three ways to get reports out of Business Central. Fabricado can't be bought yet, so its column describes what we are building. Further down: Microsoft's upcoming mirroring, and more routes.

Do the sums yourself

Against Microsoft's apps

Fabricado's route would cost our planned price plus the capacity, so SEK 4,145–5,265 more a month than Microsoft's apps. The setup fee, SEK 9,500, counts toward the first months' fees. The finance package's price isn't decided and may be higher.

If the standard reports answer your questions and someone at your company handles the updates, Microsoft's apps are cheaper and already exist.

Against a consultant

Fabricado would cost SEK 30,000 in the first year, since the setup fee counts toward the first months' fees, or SEK 49,740 with a reserved F2. At the hourly rates Brainville quotes for BI consultants on its marketplace for consulting assignments, SEK 831–944 an hour, SEK 30,000 equals about 32–36 hours, and SEK 49,740 about 53–60 hours.

How many hours a consultant needs to build and look after your reports depends on the job. Compare with a real quote.

When a consultant is the better choice

When you need reports now, when you run Business Central on your own servers, or when you want someone sitting with you.

Sources, opened on 1 November 2026: Microsoft Learn, Power BI apps by functional area (updated 22 September 2026), Microsoft Learn, Power BI apps FAQ and Brainville, hourly rates July–December 2025. The prices are on the pricing page.

Microsoft's mirroring: what it replaces, and what it doesn't

Depends on a Microsoft preview

Microsoft is building its own route from Business Central to Fabric. According to the roadmap, published on 20 October 2026, the preview is due in November 2026 and general availability in April 2027. On 6 November 2026 it hadn't been released.

What it replaces

Copying data from Business Central to Fabric. In the long run neither you, a consultant nor we need to look after that part. Microsoft also says sample Power BI apps come with it.

What it doesn't replace

The model with your measures, the reports and the check against the general ledger. Microsoft's monitor for mirroring counts rows; it doesn't compare amounts with the ledger. The guidance, the cost view and the monitoring also stay outside it.

What we do

We build without mirroring. When it is released, we build it next to the standard route, fill it, check it with Bryggan and switch only where it gives you something. Mirroring or your own data path, in detail.

Among the products and Microsoft pages we went through, we found none that checks report figures against the general ledger with a receipt. That is what we found, not proof that nobody does. Microsoft's Finance Agent has a reconciliation agent that compares two tables in Excel, not Power BI figures with the general ledger, and custom agents can pull data from Business Central.
