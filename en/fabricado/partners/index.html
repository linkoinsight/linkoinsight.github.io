For Business Central partners

Fabricado should make reports in Fabric something you can install and hand over, without a BI team of your own. Nothing can be bought yet.

How we see it working

You

Help the customer with the Microsoft steps: the capacity, approving the app and the fetch from Business Central. You are the customer's first contact.

Fabricado

Builds the data model, the reports and the check, Bryggan, in the customer's environment, and keeps them up to date when Microsoft changes something. The guide is written for the person doing the steps, one step at a time.

The customer

Approves, reads the reports and keeps everything in their own Microsoft environment.

What we can show

We show only real results from Microsoft's demo company, never sketches presented as finished. The first is Bryggan's receipt for 2025 in CRONUS SE: our copy of the ledger matches Business Central's own trial balance in all twelve months, and after the year-end close every difference is a proven closing entry. The comparison isn't per dimension yet.
