How Fabricado will work

Everything is built in your own Microsoft environment. This page describes what we are building; none of it can be bought yet.

Planned

The steps, and who does them

  1. Your Microsoft environment

    Already there

    Everyone who runs Business Central online already has a Microsoft environment (a tenant). You don't need a new one.

  2. A Fabric capacity

    You or your Business Central partner

    Fabricado builds in Microsoft Fabric, and Fabric needs a capacity. You start Microsoft's trial yourselves in Fabric, unless your administrator has turned it off, and it lasts 60 days. A paid capacity needs an Azure subscription, with a card and an identity check, and costs from SEK 1,645 a month.

    The fetch and the check against the general ledger have run on a trial. We don't know yet whether a trial is enough for all of Fabricado.

  3. Approving Fabricado's app

    Your administrator

    An administrator approves Fabricado's app in Microsoft Entra, with the permissions the app asks for. Fabricado isn't a verified publisher with Microsoft yet, so the dialog shows "Unverified".

  4. Fetching from Business Central

    Your partner or IT, with our guide

    A pipeline in your Fabric fetches the general ledger from Business Central's analytics API into a lakehouse in your environment. Sales, purchases and inventory come with their packages. It runs in your workspace, with your permissions.

  5. Data model, reports and the check against the general ledger

    Fabricado

    Fabricado installs a data model and reports in your workspace, from your browser and with the sign-in of the person who approves. The check, Bryggan, then compares the figures with the general ledger, month by month.

  6. The readers

    You

    Anyone who reads the reports needs Power BI Pro, as long as the capacity is smaller than F64, and a paid Business Central licence. Team Member is enough.

Once the app is online, it will read most things itself: accounts, capacities, settings and the access to Business Central. You only do the steps no app can click for you, and you can pause while you wait for someone.

Every day an overview shows how it went: the fetch from Business Central, the data model, the check against the general ledger and the reports, with times.

What you need

  • Business Central online. Fabricado is built for Business Central online, not for your own servers.
  • A Fabric capacity: Microsoft's trial or a paid F2.
  • Power BI Pro for every reader, as long as the capacity is smaller than F64.
  • A paid Business Central licence for every reader. Team Member is enough.
  • An administrator who can approve Fabricado's app.
  • Your Business Central partner or your IT for the fetch.

What all of it costs

The steps at Microsoft, one at a time

Every night and every morning

The fetch and the watcher will run at night, as jobs in your own workspace, so nothing depends on someone having a browser open. The watcher checks that the night's run has happened and sends status codes to an address that only receives.

  • Bryggan checks the night's figures against the general ledger and writes a receipt.
  • The overview shows whether everything is working, how fresh the figures are and what needs you.
  • If the night's run hasn't reported by 07:00, you get an email in Swedish with what happened and what to do now.
  • Temporary errors are retried on their own. When something would break the check, the flow stops and explains.

The code for the alarm and the emails exists, but nothing is sent yet: the server isn't running and no email service is connected.

When Microsoft changes something

Business Central gets two major updates a year, in April and October, and a minor update in each of the other months. This is what we promise once Fabricado can be bought:

We test every major Business Central version in Microsoft's preview environment, which opens a month before April and October. We test the smaller monthly updates on Microsoft's demo company when they are released; according to Microsoft, they usually reach your environment about a week later. If a Microsoft change still stops a report, we fix or explain it within 48 hours, and it shows on the status page.

We don't promise that the reports never break. We promise a way of working you can see:

  • Every new version of Fabricado is tested first on Microsoft's demo company.
  • Before an update, a copy of the model's definition is saved, because Fabric has no undo.
  • The new model is built next to the old one and checked with Bryggan before the reports switch over.
  • No switches during your month-end close.
  • The receipt says which version you run.
  • Everything is listed in the public changelog.

If you want to stop

The model, the reports and the receipts stay in your environment when you leave. We only stop updating them. There is no minimum term.
