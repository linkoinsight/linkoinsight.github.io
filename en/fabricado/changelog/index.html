Changelog

What changes in Fabricado, and what Microsoft changes that affects Fabricado. Both logs get an RSS feed with version 1.

Changes in Fabricado

No versions yet. The first version is written here when version 1 is released. Changes that can affect your own reports will be clearly marked, with a date.

The build so far

  • 2 November 2026

    The first test of the install

    In our trial, the administrator's approval went through and the capacity check passed. The install from the browser created all seven items, but the app couldn't read the result over Microsoft's API. That is being fixed.

  • 1 November 2026

    The first receipt through a pipeline in Fabric

    A pipeline in Fabric fetched Microsoft's demo company from Business Central, 4,361 ledger lines, and the check, Bryggan, went through 24 months. Every difference was a proven closing entry, and SEK 0.00 was unexplained.

  • 1 November 2026

    Bryggan on real data from Business Central

    All twelve months of 2025 in the demo company matched Business Central's own trial balance, on 169 accounts and 2,815 ledger lines.

When Microsoft changed something

  • 30 October 2026

    Power BI's question feature goes away in February 2027

    Microsoft is removing the question feature Q&A, where you ask a report questions in plain text. We noted it on 30 October.

    For Fabricado: we build nothing on it. Questions to your data go through your own AI. We haven't tested whether Microsoft's way of preparing a model for AI works on a Swedish model.

    Microsoft Learn on the question feature

  • 28 October 2026

    Mirroring from Business Central: Microsoft's own app becomes the default sign-in

    Microsoft changed the code for mirroring to Fabric so that customers probably won't need to create their own app secret. The change shows in the code but not yet in the documentation, and mirroring hasn't been released.

    For Fabricado: we build without mirroring, and keep the older route as a fallback until the documentation and a test in our sandbox confirm the change.

    The change on GitHub (BCApps #12557)

  • 20 October 2026

    Mirroring from Business Central gets dates on the roadmap

    Microsoft's roadmap says preview in November 2026 and general availability in April 2027. On 6 November 2026 it hadn't been released.

    For Fabricado: the copying becomes something we adopt from Microsoft instead of looking after ourselves. We test mirroring next to the standard route when it arrives. Mirroring or your own data path.

    Roadmap item RM574126
