Do we need Azure to start with Fabric?

Not to try it. A company with Business Central online already has a Microsoft environment, and Fabric's trial is started in Fabric, free for 60 days. An Azure subscription is only needed for a paid capacity, with a real card, a phone and an identity check.

Updated 6 November 2026.

What is needed when

To try it

Business Central online and an account in your own organisation. No Azure subscription, no card, SEK 0 for 60 days.

According to Microsoft the trial is meant for evaluation and testing, and Copilot and data agents can't run on it.

To run it for real

An Azure subscription and an F2, from SEK 1,645 a month with a one-year reservation, or SEK 2,765 without one if it runs all month. Virtual cards don't work.

If you have your own domain but no Microsoft environment

You then end up in an "unmanaged" environment that Azure refuses. It is rare among Business Central customers.

The steps, one at a time. What it costs.

Our own proof

We have run the whole chain on Microsoft's demo company on a trial, without buying anything: 24 months from Business Central to the receipt. We haven't tested everything real operation takes. Buying an F2 from nothing is one of the tests that remain.

What Fabricado adds

Planned

The goal is that you can start without even having Azure, with the app leading you through every step. The Azure account and the trial are still yours to do: those are steps no app can click for you.
