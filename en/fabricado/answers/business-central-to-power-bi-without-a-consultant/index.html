Answers

Can you get Business Central into Power BI without a consultant?

Yes, for standard reports. Microsoft's nine free apps for Business Central install in six steps and need no Fabric capacity, only Power BI Pro and a Business Central licence for the readers. A consultant or a ready-made product is only needed when you want more than the standard.

Updated 6 November 2026.

The routes and what they cost

Three routes from Business Central to Power BI, with costs
RouteWhat you getCost
Microsoft's free apps Ready-made reports for nine areas, including finance, sales, purchasing and inventory. You install and update them yourselves. SEK 0 for the apps. The readers' Pro: SEK 133.80 per reader a month.
A BI consultant What you order, built for you. SEK 831–944 an hour according to Brainville, July–December 2025.
Fabricado, planned Models and reports, finance first, checked against the general ledger. It can't be bought yet. Planned SEK 2,500 a month, plus a capacity for SEK 1,645–2,765.

Fabricado's route would cost SEK 4,145–5,265 more a month than the free apps. SEK 30,000 in the first year equals about 32–36 consultant hours. How many hours a consultant needs depends on the job, so compare with a real quote.

When the free apps aren't enough

  • They are built for one company at a time, each in its own workspace. Reports across companies aren't included.
  • They don't check the report's figures against the general ledger.
  • They don't show voucher and document numbers.
  • Someone at your company has to install new versions and watch that it works.

If the standard reports answer your questions and someone at your company handles the updates, choose them. They are cheaper and exist today. Microsoft also plans Fabric versions of the apps, including for several companies, but without a date.

Our own proof

We have taken Microsoft's demo company from Business Central to Fabric without a consultant. On 1 November 2026, a pipeline in Fabric loaded 4,361 ledger lines, and the check, Bryggan, went through 24 months, after the year-end close. Every difference was a proven closing entry, and SEK 0.00 was unexplained.

The same day, before the close and without Fabric, our copy of the ledger matched Business Central's own trial balance: all twelve months of 2025, 169 accounts and 2,815 ledger lines, with SEK 0.00 difference. How it was done.

We haven't built a Power BI report yet: what we have shown is the fetch and the check, not the reports. Nor have we compared with Microsoft's finance app. That comparison is coming, and the result will be here.

What Fabricado adds

Planned

Fabricado isn't an add-on to Microsoft's apps. It is models and reports of its own in Fabric, which is why it costs more. What we add is the guidance, the check against the general ledger, the overview and the updates when Microsoft changes something. The full comparison.
