What does Microsoft Fabric cost a small company?

Expect SEK 2,983–4,103 a month to Microsoft for a small company with ten report readers who already hold a Business Central licence, excluding VAT. That is an F2 capacity at SEK 1,645 with a one-year reservation, or SEK 2,765 without one if it runs all month, plus Power BI Pro at SEK 133.80 per reader. Microsoft's trial is free for 60 days.

Updated 6 November 2026.

What makes the bill bigger or smaller

  • Pausing. Without a reservation you only pay for the hours the capacity runs. A night run needs it running at night.
  • Overage. According to Microsoft's page on overage (29 September 2026), it is on by default, while the same page calls it opt-in, and its threshold isn't a hard spending cap. It bills at three times the pay-as-you-go rate. Check the setting on your own capacity.
  • The dollar rate. Microsoft sets the capacity's price in US dollars and converts it every month, so the amount in kronor can change.
  • Storage. $0.023 per gigabyte a month. Mirrored data is free up to 2 terabytes on an F2.
  • F64. Readers can do without Pro only at F64, which costs about SEK 52,600 a month with a one-year reservation.
  • F0. Microsoft has announced a preview of a size with no fixed base charge, where you pay per capacity hour used. It could change the cheapest setup, but we don't know yet what can run on it.
  • AI. Copilot and other AI features at Microsoft can cost more and aren't included above.

What we have measured ourselves

We have run the whole chain from Business Central to the receipt on Microsoft's trial, without buying anything. That trial was an F64, so we haven't measured whether an F2 is enough. We expect it to be for one company, but that is an expectation, not a result. We will write it here once it is measured.

Be careful with kronor per job, including from us: a job is a share of a fixed capacity bill. A faster job only lowers the invoice if the capacity can shrink or pause.

What Fabricado adds

Planned

We plan SEK 2,500 a month per Business Central company on top of Microsoft's share. With ten readers who already hold a Business Central licence, the total is SEK 5,483–6,603 a month. The full calculation. If you don't need Fabric at all, there is a cheaper answer: Microsoft's free apps.
