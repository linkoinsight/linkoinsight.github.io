Answers about Business Central, Fabric and Power BI

Straight answers with figures, dates and sources. Where we have results of our own from Microsoft's demo company, they are included. The answers hold even if you never buy anything from us.

The questions

  • What does Microsoft Fabric cost?

    About SEK 2,983–4,103 a month to Microsoft with ten readers.

  • Business Central to Power BI without a consultant

    Yes, for standard reports. More than the standard takes a consultant or a ready-made product.

  • Before you start with Fabric

    Five things: a Microsoft environment, a capacity, an administrator, access to Business Central and licences.

  • Mirroring or your own data path

    Today only your own data path exists. Mirroring replaces the copying, but not the model or the check.

  • Does the report match the ledger?

    Compare with Business Central's own trial balance, per account and month, and explain the differences with proof.

  • Who needs Power BI Pro?

    Everyone who reads reports, as long as the capacity is smaller than F64. SEK 133.80 per reader a month.

  • Do we need Azure?

    Not to try it. An Azure subscription is only needed for a paid capacity.

The steps at Microsoft, one at a time, are under Help.
