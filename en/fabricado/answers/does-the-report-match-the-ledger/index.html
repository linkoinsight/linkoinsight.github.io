How do you check that a report matches the general ledger?

Compare the figures with Business Central's own trial balance, per account and month, and explain every difference with proof. The answer key must be independent: a check against the same data that fed the model can't see its own mistakes.

Updated 6 November 2026.

How it is done

  1. Fetch the answer key

    Read Business Central's trial balance per company and date. It is the books' own answer, independent of your report.

  2. Sum your copy the same way

    Work out the period's net change and closing balance per account and month.

  3. Compare account by account

    The difference should be SEK 0.00. Anything else is a difference that has to be explained.

  4. Explain only with proof

    A closing entry is recognised by its Source Code field and its date. If a difference can't be proven it is unexplained, and then the figures don't match.

  5. Keep the result

    A receipt with a date, a scope and a seal lets you show the check afterwards.

The traps in Business Central

  • The trial balance leaves the closing entries out of December. So a December difference after the year-end close doesn't have to be an error.
  • Closing entries must be recognised by the Source Code field and the date, never by one hard-coded value. There may be several such codes, or none.
  • The balance sheet must never be date-filtered at the source: the opening balances then come out wrong.
  • The fields "Finance Start Date" and "Finance End Date" in Power BI Reports Setup cut rows away without showing it. They must be blank.
  • Check against a source outside the model. An earlier version of ours compared the model with the same data that fed it, and so couldn't see its own mistakes.

Our own proof

In Microsoft's demo company, our copy of the general ledger matches Business Central's trial balance for all twelve months of 2025: 169 accounts, 2,815 ledger lines, SEK 0.00 difference. We fetched the same year from both the analytics API and API v2.0.

After the year-end close, the check ran through a pipeline in Fabric over 24 months and 4,361 ledger lines. Every difference was a proven closing entry, and SEK 0.00 was unexplained.

That is as far as it goes: the comparison is per account and month, not per dimension, and the reports' own figures aren't checked, because the reports don't exist yet.
