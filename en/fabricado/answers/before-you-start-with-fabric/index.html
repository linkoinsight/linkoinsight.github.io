What do you need before you start with Microsoft Fabric?

Five things: a Microsoft environment, a Fabric capacity, an administrator who can approve apps, access to Business Central and licences for those who will read. If you run Business Central online you already have the first, and you start the trial yourselves with one click.

Updated 6 November 2026.

The list

What is needed before you start with Fabric, who does it and what it costs
What is neededWho does itCost
A Microsoft environment Already there if you run Business Central online. None.
A Fabric capacity You or your partner. The steps. The trial is free for 60 days. An F2 costs from SEK 1,645 a month.
An administrator who can approve apps Your Global administrator, often at your partner. How it works. None.
Access to Business Central Your partner or IT. What it takes. None beyond what you already pay.
Licences for the readers Whoever handles your Microsoft licences. Which ones. SEK 133.80 per reader a month for Pro, plus SEK 76.50 if there is no Business Central licence.

In a small company, one person may hold all of these roles.

Three traps

The partner signs in as a guest

A guest can't be a capacity administrator. Sign in with an account in the customer's own organisation.

The company has its own domain but no Microsoft environment

It then ends up in an "unmanaged" environment that Azure refuses. It is rare among Business Central customers, but it happens.

The organisation is too new

Microsoft can refuse the trial. See below.

What happened to us

When we went to start the trial in a newly created organisation on 1 November 2026, Microsoft answered "A Fabric trial isn't available for your account". The likely cause is that the organisation was too new. Support answers on Microsoft Q&A say about 90 days, but Microsoft doesn't document it. We ran on a trial in another organisation instead, and there it worked.

What Fabricado adds

Planned

A check that reads most of this itself instead of asking you: the account, the approval, the capacity, the settings and the access to Business Central. That part is built into the app, but the app isn't online. It reads licences only for the person signed in, and the price and the Business Central version aren't built. Today it takes about ten steps across three roles, and we want to shorten that.
