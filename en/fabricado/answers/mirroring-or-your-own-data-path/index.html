Mirroring or your own data path from Business Central to Fabric?

Today only your own data path exists: Microsoft's mirroring isn't released. According to the roadmap, the preview is due in November 2026 and general availability in April 2027. Once released, it replaces the copying, but not the model, the reports or the check against the general ledger.

Updated 6 November 2026.

Side by side

Microsoft's mirroring compared with your own data path
Microsoft's mirroring Your own data path
Status on 6 November 2026 Not released. The roadmap says preview in November 2026 and general availability in April 2027. Usable today: the analytics API and Fabric's tools are generally available.
What lands in Fabric Business Central's raw tables, up to 1,000 of them. What the API gives: ledger entries, trial balance, chart of accounts and dimensions.
Needed in Business Central Online 29.0 or later according to Microsoft's pages, but the code we read needs 29.1. The Microsoft Fabric Mirroring extension, and data encryption turned on. Microsoft's extension for the Power BI reports.
Capacity An active Fabric capacity. Whether the trial is enough is untested. A Fabric capacity. The trial works.
Sign-in The documentation says an app registration of your own with a secret. The code changed on 28 October so that Microsoft's own app becomes the default, but that isn't confirmed. An app registration with a secret that lasts 12 months at most.
Pausing Start, stop and reset are documented. The pipeline runs when you decide.
  • Mirrored data is free up to 2 terabytes on an F2.
  • According to a Business Central MVP, mirroring doesn't use up the API limits.
  • Dimensions 3–8 aren't mirrored, according to our review.
  • If a secret expires the copying stops, on either route.

What mirroring replaces, and what it doesn't

Replaces

Copying data to Fabric. Microsoft also says sample Power BI apps come with it.

Doesn't replace

The model with your measures, the reports, the check against the general ledger and the monitoring. Microsoft's monitor for mirroring counts rows; it doesn't compare amounts with the ledger. Raw tables aren't finished measures either: someone has to work out the trial balance.

Our own proof

We didn't wait. On 1 November 2026, a pipeline in Fabric loaded Microsoft's demo company from the analytics API, 4,361 ledger lines, and the check, Bryggan, went through 24 months with SEK 0.00 unexplained. That route works today.

What we do when mirroring arrives

Depends on a Microsoft preview

We build it next to the standard route, fill it, check it with Bryggan and switch only where it gives you something, with the same measurements before and after: time to fresh figures, capacity and cost, and which fields come along. It is the first real test of how Fabricado handles a change at Microsoft. The full comparison.

Sources

Roadmap item RM574126 (20 October 2026), Microsoft on setting up mirroring (prerelease documentation), Microsoft on start, stop and reset, the code change in BCApps #12557 and Microsoft's connector for Business Central.
