Who needs Power BI Pro with Microsoft Fabric?

Everyone who reads reports, as long as the capacity is smaller than F64. That applies on an F2 too: the capacity doesn't replace the readers' licences. From F64 on, readers can do without Pro, but F64 costs about SEK 52,600 a month with a one-year reservation.

Updated 6 November 2026.

The figures

Whether readers need Power BI Pro, per capacity
CapacityDo readers need Pro?
F2 to F32 Yes. SEK 133.80 per reader a month, SEK 1,338 for ten readers.
F64 and larger No, but the capacity costs about SEK 52,600 a month with a one-year reservation.
The trial Assume Pro is needed, unless the trial is an F64. It can also be an F4, and Microsoft decides which.

Microsoft writes that on F SKUs smaller than F64, each user viewing Power BI content must have Pro, Premium Per User or an individual trial. Anyone reading Business Central data in Power BI also needs a paid Business Central licence. Team Member is enough, at SEK 76.50 per reader a month.

What is unclear

Can you avoid Pro by embedding the reports in an app of your own on an F2? Microsoft's pages disagree: one lists it under external users, another says "anyone inside or outside the organization". The route also needs a stored service key. So we never promise that Pro isn't needed without written confirmation from Microsoft.

What we do with this

We don't charge per reader, but Microsoft does, and with ten readers Microsoft's bill is larger than our planned fee. So we show it next to our own. We went through the vendors in October 2026 and found no one else who did.
