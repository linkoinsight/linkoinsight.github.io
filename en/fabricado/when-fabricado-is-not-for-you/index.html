When Fabricado isn't for you

Better that you know now than after a month of setup.

Choose something else if

You need the reports now

Fabricado can't be bought yet. Microsoft's own Power BI apps and consultants are available today.

You run Business Central on your own servers

Fabricado is built for Business Central online. Microsoft's Power BI apps are also online only.

You don't have Business Central

Fabricado starts with Business Central. Other business systems are in the plan, but without a date.

The standard reports are enough

Then Microsoft's own apps are cheaper: Fabricado's route costs SEK 4,145–5,265 more a month.

You don't want to pay for a Fabric capacity

The first version of Fabricado needs one, from SEK 1,645 a month. Microsoft's apps manage with Power BI Pro.

You have your own BI team

Fabricado is made for companies without a BI team. A team that already builds in Fabric gets less out of it.

You want phone or chat support

Support will be by email, with an answer within one working day. There will be no phone and no chat.

You require ISO 27001, SOC 2 or a verified Microsoft publisher

Fabricado has none of these today.

You want the supplier to run everything in your environment

Fabricado is built to hold no keys to your environment. What is done there is done by you, your partner or from your browser.

You may not use AI that processes data outside the EU

Then don't turn on changes in plain words when they arrive. The check, Bryggan, is ordinary code without AI.
