Pricing

SEK 2,500 to us. SEK 2,983 to Microsoft.

A month, excluding VAT, for a Business Central company with ten readers who already hold a Business Central licence, and a Fabric capacity reserved for a year. Without the reservation, Microsoft charges SEK 4,103. The setup fee, SEK 9,500, counts toward the first months' fees if you continue.

This is the price we plan. Fabricado can't be bought yet, and the price may change before it can.

More readers only raise Microsoft's share: SEK 133.80 per reader a month for Power BI Pro. If the readers have no Business Central licence, Team Member adds SEK 76.50 per reader a month. With ten such readers, the total is SEK 6,248–7,368.

The setup fee

Setup will cost SEK 9,500, excluding VAT, including the check, Bryggan, on your own figures. It is invoiced only when Bryggan matches on your figures, or when every difference is explained. If you continue after setup, the SEK 9,500 counts toward the first months' fees.

Good to know about Microsoft's share

  • The capacity is bought in Azure. There, Microsoft sets the price in US dollars and converts it to kronor every month, so the amount in kronor can change.
  • The reservation commits you to Microsoft for a year. Without it you pay by the hour, and SEK 2,765 applies if the capacity runs all month.
  • We expect one F2 to be enough for one company. That isn't tested yet: our run was on a larger trial capacity.
  • Power BI Pro and the Business Central licences are billed yearly by Microsoft.
  • Readers can do without Pro only when the capacity is F64 or larger. F64 costs about SEK 52,600 a month with a one-year reservation.
  • Microsoft's AI features, such as Copilot, can cost more and aren't included here.
  • Microsoft's Fabric trial is free for 60 days and, according to Microsoft, meant for evaluation and testing. The fetch and the check have run on a trial. We don't know yet whether a trial is enough for all of Fabricado.

Sources, checked on 1 November 2026: Microsoft's prices for Power BI, Microsoft's prices for Business Central, Microsoft's prices for Fabric (Sweden Central, from Azure's price list in kronor), Microsoft on Fabric licences and on the trial.

What the price includes, once Fabricado can be bought

  • The model and the reports in the first thing you can buy, installed in your environment.
  • Bryggan after every refresh, with a receipt.
  • Updated packages when Microsoft changes something.
  • The overview, cost against your limit, and alerts.
  • Support by email.
  • All readers. We don't charge per reader.

The finance package's price isn't decided and may be higher than SEK 2,500. We will set it once the package exists.

What it doesn't include

  • Microsoft's capacity and licences, see above.
  • More packages. What they will cost isn't decided.
  • Work by your Business Central partner.
  • Changes in Business Central.

Whether changes in plain words will be part of the price isn't decided.

Several companies, and the terms

If you have several Business Central companies, companies two and three will cost SEK 1,250 a month each. From the fourth company on you pay nothing more, so you pay us at most SEK 5,000 a month.

The plan is no minimum term and no annual contract. All prices are in Swedish kronor, excluding VAT.

Compared with consultant hours

The first year of Fabricado would cost SEK 30,000: twelve months at SEK 2,500, since the setup fee counts toward the first months' fees. With a reserved F2 it is SEK 49,740. For BI consultants in Sweden, Brainville, a marketplace for consulting assignments, quotes SEK 944 an hour as the supplier rate and SEK 831 as the buyer rate, for July to December 2025. At those rates, SEK 49,740 equals about 53 to 60 consultant hours.

How many hours a consultant needs depends on the job, so compare with a real quote. The readers' Microsoft licences are needed either way.
