Security and data

A finance manager or IT lead asks hard questions before anything is connected to the books. Here are our answers, and how far they hold today. Nothing can be bought yet.

Three promises, and how far they go

Built in your own Microsoft environment

Planned

The model, the reports and the receipts from the check, Bryggan, will be built in your own Microsoft environment, in your own Fabric workspace.

Fabricado's own service should store as little as possible. We publish the full list once it is tested in our sandbox with a real sign-in.

No keys or passwords to your environment are handed to us

Planned

You sign in with your own Microsoft account, and Microsoft asks for your consent first. What Fabricado installs is done from your browser, with the sign-in of the person who approves, and the fetch and the watcher run as jobs in your own workspace. The watcher checks that the night's run has happened and sends status codes to an address that only receives.

The limit: our code in the browser reaches what the signed-in person reaches. That is why the app will talk only to Microsoft and Fabricado, and why every version will get a published checksum your IT can compare with.

Bryggan calculates with ordinary code

Proven on demo data

The comparison with the general ledger is ordinary, tested code, not an AI model. The same figures give the same comparison and the same explanations every time.

Bryggan's first step, our copy of the ledger against Business Central's trial balance, matches for all twelve months of 2025 in Microsoft's demo company, and after the year-end close every difference is a proven closing entry. The comparison isn't per dimension yet. Where else AI is used is set out further down.

Where things will be

Where Fabricado's parts will be
Place What will be there
Your Microsoft environment Business Central. The copy of the general ledger and sales, in a lakehouse in your Fabric. The model and the reports. Bryggan's receipts, and a copy of the model before every change. The fetch and the watcher, which run at night as jobs in your workspace.
Your browser Fabricado's app, signed in as you. It reads and changes your environment with your permissions.
Fabricado's service, in Microsoft Azure in Sweden Accounts and billing. The service should store as little as possible, and we publish the list once it is tested in our sandbox.

Microsoft is a US company, and US law such as the CLOUD Act can apply to what is stored in Azure, in Sweden too. That is why we want as little as possible to be stored with us.

If you leave, or if we disappear

What stays

The model, the reports and the receipts stay in your environment. We stop updating them.

How to switch Fabricado off

Remove Fabricado's app in Microsoft Entra, and Fabricado's jobs in your workspace. Then Fabricado can't do anything more in your environment.

AI, sub-processors and agreements

Changes in plain words, the sub-processors and the agreement below are planned.

Changes in plain words

If you turn them on, an AI proposes the change from how the model is built, without rows from your tables. A named person at your company approves before anything changes. The plan is Anthropic's Claude, and processing may then take place outside the EU, since Claude has no EU data residency today. Where the AI will run for customers isn't decided yet.

Support answers

AI may help us write answers to your emails. When it does, the answer says so.

AI at Microsoft

Copilot and other AI at Microsoft are governed by your settings at Microsoft, not by Fabricado.

New tenants in the EU may already have Claude switched on in Copilot for Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and processing then takes place outside the EU. Check your settings.

Sub-processors

Microsoft, for hosting in Azure. Anthropic, for changes in plain words if they are turned on, and when AI helps us with support answers. An email service and a payment service, neither chosen yet.

Data processing agreement

We will offer every customer a data processing agreement based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

What we don't have yet

  • No security certification, such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2.
  • No external penetration test.
  • Fabricado isn't a verified publisher with Microsoft.
  • No status page.
  • No final terms and no final data processing agreement.

This site loads nothing from other websites and sets no cookies. Your IT can see that for themselves in the browser's developer tools, under network.
