Fabricado, from Linko Insight

All the way to reports that add up.

For companies that run Business Central and don't know what to do, what it costs or how it's done.

Fabricado will take you from there to finished models and reports in Power BI, in your own Microsoft environment, checked against the general ledger. The first proof already exists, right here.

Get in touch See how far we have come

Four questions most companies get stuck on

What should we do?

Available

The help pages show the steps at Microsoft, who does what and which errors you may run into. In the app, a check will read your environment and say what is missing; that part is planned.

What does it cost?

Available

We show Microsoft's bill next to our own. A company with ten readers who already hold a Business Central licence would pay SEK 5,483–6,603 a month, of which SEK 2,500 to us. The full calculation.

How do we do it?

Planned

Fabricado will install everything in your own Microsoft environment, from your browser. The goal is plug and play: you click and read, nobody writes code. Today it takes about ten steps across three roles, and the app isn't online. How it will work.

Do the figures add up?

Proven on demo data

In Microsoft's demo company, our copy of the general ledger matches Business Central's own trial balance for all twelve months of 2025, with SEK 0.00 difference. The reports' own figures are the next step, once the reports exist. How to check it.

What Fabricado is meant to be

  • All the way

    Planned

    From not knowing where to start to finished reports and a daily overview, without a BI team of your own.

  • Transparency

    Available

    We show what exists, what is planned and when Fabricado isn't for you. The receipt is here as a file.

  • Cost without surprises

    Available

    The cost table with Microsoft's share is here today. Cost against your own limit, and measurements of what a change actually gave, are planned.

  • Models and reports

    Planned

    The model and the measures matter as much as the reports: they are what make the reports and your own AI calculate alike. None of it is built yet. The packages.

  • Health, monitoring and alarms

    Planned

    Clear views of the data: how fresh it is, whether it adds up and what needs you. An email when something has stopped. The code for the alarm exists, but no alarm is sent yet.

Finance first, then sales, purchases and inventory

Planned

Each package is a model, reports and a receipt of its own where Business Central has a figure to check against. If you need something the package doesn't have, such as stock turnover, you should be able to ask for it in plain words, see the change in Swedish, approve it and get a receipt.
